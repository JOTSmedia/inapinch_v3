IN A PINCH — V3 / 24 CITIES

27,431 mapped places. City-specific cartographic vector maps.
V1 and V2 are preserved in their original folders.

QUICK PREVIEW:
Double-click OPEN_ME.html. Keep the assets folder beside it.
Use a current browser; maps and discovery require internet access.
Automatic location works best on HTTPS or localhost.

GITHUB UPLOAD:
Upload the CONTENTS of this folder, preserving subfolders.
index.html should appear directly in the repository root.
Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root).
You can also upload the folder as /v3/ to retain older versions.

NO MAP API KEY IS REQUIRED.

Current-location startup, 24 selectable city maps, Wireframe / City modes,
numbered pins, filters, saved places, walking directions, and Google Maps
business links are included. Zoom into neighborhoods to discover more venues.

Coverage is not exhaustive. A mapped restaurant or bar does not guarantee
bathroom access. Unknown access and amenities are labeled conservatively.
Shared community ratings require the included backend.

README.md includes coverage details, source dates, city totals and maintenance.
